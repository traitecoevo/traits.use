# traits.use: Access and Wrangle Data from 'traits.build' Trait Databases

Load, explore and wrangle data from relational trait databases built
with 'traits.build' <https://github.com/traitecoevo/traits.build>, such
as AusTraits, the Australian plant trait database (Falster et al. 2021
[doi:10.1038/s41597-021-01006-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-021-01006-6)
). Functions load a database from its DOI, join data across its tables,
filter it to specific taxa, traits or datasets, combine several
databases, look up references, and visualise the distribution of the
data.
